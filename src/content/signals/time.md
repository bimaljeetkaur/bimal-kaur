---
title: "If Time Had Stopped"
date: "25 OCtober 2026"
category: ["Poetry"]
description: "Random word jumble or maybe a poem"
---

Why did we meet so untimely?

Why did I not stop myself sooner,  
before I reached out to you  
again and again?

And yet,  
there was nothing between us anyway.

Sometimes, I want to ask—  
what if time had stopped?  
Would ours have stopped, too?  
Or has our time together  
already come to an end?

And yet, time and again,  
I imagine us crossing paths by chance.

My eyes meeting yours.  
A thousand questions  
passing between us  
in a fraction of a second.

What if I had become yours?  
What if God had intervened  
and made you mine?

Would you have surrendered to His will,  
or rebelled against it,  
and walked away as you did before?

Would you have left my prayers unanswered,  
shattered my dreams  
of crossing the distance  
that kept me from reaching you?

And would you, one last time,
deliver your final punishment—
erase you from my memory,
undo the day our paths first crossed,
return me to the life I knew
before I ever knew you?

So I could live and breathe again,
without the weight of what might have been,
without the ache of what never was.

And perhaps,
if I had never met you,
I would never have known this sorrow.

I could have walked beneath the sun,
unburdened,
unbroken,
and never wondered
why its warmth no longer felt the same.
